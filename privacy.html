Privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026.

KH Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses

  • Permission to upload videos and thumbnails to the channel the owner approves (youtube.upload).
  • Read-only access to that channel's basic details, only to confirm that uploads go to the right channel (youtube.readonly).

How the data is used and kept

  • It only uploads the owner's own videos and thumbnails, as Private.
  • The access permission is stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is stored on any server, shared, sold, or used for advertising or any other purpose.

How to remove access

Access can be removed at any time in your Google Account under Security → Third-party apps and services, or at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
